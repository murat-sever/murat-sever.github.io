## What You Will Learn

By the end of this course, attendees will have a solid intuitive understanding of:

- **GNU Radio** — flowgraph creation, blocks, data types, and scheduler
- **DSP fundamentals** — sampling, aliasing, digital filtering, decimation, and interpolation
- **SDR concepts** — architectures, RTL-SDR hardware, and general-purpose SDR applications
- **Analog modulation** — NBFM and WBFM demodulation including stereo, RDS, and subchannels
- **Digital modulation** — QPSK simulation, constellation diagrams, carrier and timing synchronization
- **ADS-B decoding** — extracting flight data from aircraft transponder signals

## Target Audience

This course is designed for engineers, professionals, students, and academics interested in signal processing or wireless communications. Whether you are new to the field or an experienced DSP engineer looking for a fresh, practical perspective — this course offers immediate, applicable value.

## Course Format & Materials

All hardware (RTL-SDR) is provided. Materials include:

- GNU Radio flowgraphs
- Slides and walkthrough examples
- IQ recordings
- Capture The Signal (CTS) session
