Learning wireless communications has never been this easy and fun! This course aims to teach communication systems by experiencing them firsthand through Software Defined Radios. Live radio frequency signals are analyzed using open-source software tools, with practical decoding examples throughout.

<Aside type="tip">
  No prior RF experience needed. Basic computer knowledge and general Python familiarity are sufficient to get started.
</Aside>

## What You Will Learn

By the end of this course, attendees will have a solid intuitive understanding of:

- **RF fundamentals** — frequency, bandwidth, wavelength, RF spectrum, and modulation
- **RTL-SDR hardware** — architecture, key parameters, and setup
- **Spectrum monitoring** — observing commercial FM, airband, ISM, mobile networks, and ADS-B
- **Analog modulation** — NBFM and WBFM demodulation with real-world examples
- **ADS-B decoding** — extracting flight data from aircraft transponder signals
- **GNU Radio** — introduction to flowgraph-based signal processing

## Target Audience

This course is designed for engineers, professionals, students, and academics who are curious about wireless communications and the RF spectrum. No prior RF or SDR experience is required — just a passion for learning and exploring the invisible world of radio waves.

## Course Format & Materials

All hardware (RTL-SDR) is provided. Materials include:

- Slides and walkthrough examples
- GNU Radio flowgraphs
- IQ recordings
- Capture The Signal (CTS) sessions
