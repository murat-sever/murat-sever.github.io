This classroom training course provides a comprehensive overview of mobile communications — from RF fundamentals and digital signal processing all the way to building real 2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G networks with an SDR and a PC. Topics are brought to life through live instructor-led demonstrations and hands-on exercises using **Software Defined Radios (SDR)**.

<Aside type="tip">
  No programming skills required. Basic computer usage is sufficient to attend this course.
</Aside>

## What You Will Learn

Upon completion, participants will have a solid understanding of:

- **RF fundamentals** — frequency bands, spectrum allocations, radio architecture
- **DSP concepts** — sampling, aliasing, Nyquist, digital filtering, multirate signal processing, FFT
- **SDR concepts and architectures** — RTL-SDR, HackRF, GNU Radio toolkit
- **Analog modulation** — CW, AM, NBFM, WBFM
- **Digital modulation** — PAM, ASK, PSK, QAM, OFDM, spread spectrum
- **Wireless channel effects** — noise, multipath, Doppler shift, link budget, FEC
- **Digital communications** — pulse shaping, synchronization, eye diagram, constellation diagram
- **Cellular networks** — GSM, UMTS, LTE, 5G architectures and hands-on network building
- **Security** — Replay Attack concepts and live demonstration with FlipperZero, URH, rtl_433

## Hands-on Activities

This course is packed with practical sessions:

- RF "Hello World" with RTL-SDR
- Signal exploration with GNU Radio
- Stereo sound production and sound processing
- RF spectrum viewing (fosphor) and painting (gr-paint)
- Analog signal demodulation (AM, LSB/USB, NBFM, WBFM, CW)
- End-to-end digital communication simulation (QPSK)
- Capture the Signal (CTS) sessions
- Replay Attack demo with FlipperZero, URH, rtl_433, GNU Radio
- Building and analysing 2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G networks

## Target Audience

This course appeals to engineers from all disciplines. Computer scientists, programmers, and mechanical, automotive, and aerospace engineers — in addition to electrical and electronics engineers — will all benefit from this deep dive into modern telecommunications technology.

## Course Format & Materials

All hardware (RTL-SDR, HackRF, walkie-talkies, mobile phones, and other RF equipment) is provided. Materials include:

- Lecture slides
- GNU Radio flowgraphs
- IQ recordings
- Capture The Signal (CTS) sessions
- Replay Attack demonstration
