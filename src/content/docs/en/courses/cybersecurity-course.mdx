  No prior RF or SDR experience needed. Basic computer knowledge and general Python familiarity are sufficient to get started.

## What You Will Learn

By the end of this course, attendees will have a solid intuitive understanding of:

- Core RF principles, digital modulation techniques, and SDR architecture design
- Hands-on operation of SDR hardware to capture, process, and demodulate signals
- Spectrum monitoring, signal scanning, and RF anomaly detection
- Constructing custom DSP pipelines using GNU Radio
- Practical RF transmission mechanics and cyber attack methodologies
- Executing a comprehensive capstone project to replay attack on RF remote using SDR.

## Hands-On Capstone Exercise
Participants will apply course methodologies to capture, analyze, reverse engineer, and exploit an RF remote in a controlled environment. 

## Target Audience
This course is designed for engineers, professionals, students, and academics interested in signal processing or wireless communications. Whether you are new to the field or an experienced DSP engineer looking for a fresh, practical perspective — this course offers immediate, applicable value.

## Course Format & Materials

All hardware (HackRF One) is provided. Materials include:

- Jupyter Notebooks
- GNU Radio flowgraphs
- Slides and walkthrough examples
- IQ recordings
